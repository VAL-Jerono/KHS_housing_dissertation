🏠 What is the HFVS score?Ni nini Alama ya HFVS?

The Housing Financial Vulnerability Score (HFVS) measures how hard it is for families in your county to afford safe, secure housing. It combines five problems into one number between 0 and 1. A score closer to 1 means more hardship.

Alama ya Udhaifu wa Fedha za Makazi (HFVS) inapima jinsi ilivyo vigumu kwa familia kupata makazi salama. Inaunganisha matatizo matano katika nambari moja kati ya 0 na 1. Alama karibu na 1 inamaanisha ugumu zaidi.

Financial StressMsongo wa Fedha

Tenure InsecurityKukosa Haki ya Ardhi

D3
Physical HazardHatari ya Mazingira

Dwelling QualityUbora wa Makazi
Grass or leaking iron-sheet roofs, mud walls, earthen floors, and too many people in too small a space.Paa za nyasi au bati zinazovuja, kuta za udongo, sakafu ya udongo, na watu wengi katika nafasi ndogo.

📐 The HFVS FormulaFomula ya HFVS
HFVS_i = (D1_i + D2_i + D3_i + D4_i + D5_i) / 5    [equal weights, n = 21,347]
Each dimension is normalised to [0,1] via z-score standardisation followed by min-max rescaling, then averaged equally. County estimates are survey-weighted using the KNBS sampling design (ratio 327.6×). Kila kipimo kinasanifishwa hadi [0,1] kupitia usanifishaji wa z-score ikifuatiwa na upimaji wa min-max, kisha wastani sawa. Makadirio ya kaunti yana uzito wa utafiti wa KNBS (uwiano 327.6×).
📏 Five Dimensions — Full SpecificationVipimo Vitano — Maelezo Kamili
D1 — Financial Stress (Effective weight: 23.7%)
Rent-to-expenditure burden (>30% threshold), absence of formal savings, no formal/informal loan access. Key stat: 96.8% have no formal loan instrument. Mzigo wa kodi kwa matumizi (>30%), kukosa akiba rasmi, hakuna mkopo. Takwimu: Asilimia 96.8 hawana chombo chochote cha mkopo rasmi.
D2 — Tenure Insecurity (Effective weight: 13.8%)
Land ownership status, written lease presence, active parcel disputes, informal settlement indicator. Key stat: 54.5% report no land ownership nationally. Hali ya umiliki wa ardhi, mkataba wa maandishi, migogoro ya viwanja. Takwimu: Asilimia 54.5 hawana umiliki wa ardhi.
D3 — Physical Hazard (Effective weight: 6.2%)
Flood zone designation, mudslide zone exposure. Note: six proximity sub-columns absent from the 2023/24 extract suppressed D3. Key stats: 19.1% in flood zones; 12.6% in mudslide zones. Eneo la mafuriko, maporomoko ya udongo. Kumbuka: vipande sita vya ukaribu havikuwepo, kupunguza uzito wa D3. Takwimu: 19.1% katika maeneo ya mafuriko.
D4 — Dwelling Quality (Effective weight: 22.0%)
Roof/wall/floor material durability, occupancy density (>2 persons/room). Key stat: 38.2% of households are overcrowded. Uimara wa nyenzo za paa/kuta/sakafu, msongamano wa kukaa. Takwimu: Asilimia 38.2 wamezidi watu.
D5 — Utility Deprivation (Effective weight: 34.3%)
Grid electricity, cooking fuel (solid = deprived), improved water source, improved sanitation. Dominates due to near-universal solid-fuel cooking (96.6%) and limited grid access (43.7% unconnected). Umeme wa gridi, mafuta ya kupikia, maji bora, usafi. Inatawala kutokana na upikaji wa kuni (96.6%) na upatikanaji mdogo wa umeme (43.7% hawajaunganishwa).
📊 Statistical ValidationUthibitisho wa Takwimu
All five dimension scores are significantly non-normal (Shapiro-Wilk p<0.001; KS p<0.001 for all). Parametric and nonparametric methods were run in parallel; all substantive decisions agree.

Urban households score higher than rural (Welch t=9.78, d=0.12; Mann-Whitney r=0.067). Female-headed households face elevated vulnerability (t=5.21, d=0.07). Education gradient exists (ANOVA η²=0.015). All effect sizes are small — the HFVS is not reducible to demographic proxies.

The proxy ML model (isotonic-calibrated LightGBM-XGBoost blend) achieves AUC=0.782 and Brier=0.186 on held-out data, with county-level Spearman ρ=0.960. The Brier score falls below the 0.22 actuarial threshold, enabling direct premium loading applications. Alama zote za vipimo vitano haziko katika usambazaji wa kawaida (Shapiro-Wilk p<0.001). Njia za parametric na non-parametric zilitekelezwa sambamba.

Kaya za mijini zinapata alama za juu zaidi kuliko vijijini (Welch t=9.78, d=0.12). Mfano wa ML unafikia AUC=0.782 na Brier=0.186 — chini ya kizingiti cha 0.22 cha uaktuwali.
